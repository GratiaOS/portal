Written, not built: [RFC 001](docs/rfc-001-presence-physics.md), a draft of rooms where presence is rhythm and space and leaves no residue (`breath`, `quiet`, `practical`, `open`); it is for discussion, and nothing in it is implemented. Its section 17 says where our two rooms lean, and why no `type` goes into a config until the hub can keep it: the RFC assumes a WebSocket and a signal with no identity, the portal streams over SSE and keeps presence by participant. What is left of section 11 in the present rooms (the named dots, the count in the tab's title, the clock on a message) is weighed one by one, and none of it needs a WebSocket.

Live: [RFC 002](docs/rfc-002-drop.md), a one-way box on a frequency (`drop`), since 2026-11-18 (`0.5.0`). Amigo drops a signal with no sender and no key, the portal's box is for the holder of the frequency (no Reply, no thread; a packet is closed or **burned**, for good), and a packet fades, from the file too. On `firegate.space` the config has a `drops` block with a frequency for each of the ten people and `https://amigo.sh` in `allowOrigins`; Amigo (amigo.sh, deployed the same day) lets `FREQ:` be tuned to a frequency, and on a frequency a signal leaves by `Send` and by nothing else, as on `@garden` it leaves by `Share` and `Copy`. **Looked at, live:** a preflight from amigo.sh (`204`, with the headers a browser needs) and from another origin (`403`, with none); a drop to a held and to an unheld frequency (the same `204`, with the same headers); an origin that is not listed (`403`), a frequency badly written (`400`), a body over 8 KB (`413`), the read with no key (`401`); one drop sent from amigo.sh in a browser, from `?freq=` to the confirmation, and a person of the team opening their box on `firegate.space` with their key, who said it was fine (what they saw, point by point, was not asked). **Not looked at:** a phone, the Cloudflare alarm for a fade run there (it is checked as the time it is set to), a packet that fades while open, the keyboard and a screen reader on the box and on the tuner. The numbers (8 KB, 15 a minute per address and frequency with a burst of 3, 60 a minute per frequency, 50 alive) are starting values, not measured ones, and the Romanian and Spanish of Amigo's new words are to be read by someone who speaks them.

Only talked about, not lined up: other kinds of room than a team's and a visitors' (voice, a canvas), a way for a program outside the process to listen to a room (`docs/how-it-works.md` says where it would touch), and a signal that each portal says of itself rather than one for the repository.

A portal that opens a guest room needs its own config for it (and an engine, if the companion is on): that is the config of whoever runs it, not a matter of this repository.

## Known limits

- On Cloudflare, after a deploy or a change of a secret, the object that is the portal can answer with the previous code or config for up to five minutes (Cloudflare's default deferred update, 300 seconds): look once, after the minutes. The `wrangler` pinned here has no flag to apply a code update at once; a newer one does (INSTALL.md).
- Closing the tab is not leaving: a visit runs out its time, as before. A landscape phone (375 px high) puts "Run your own" below the fold.
- Taking a message out sooner, on request, is not built: messages are kept until the portal forgets them, and nothing else removes one.
- A person with no `locale` gets the page in their browser's language and Nova's fixed sentences in English; there is no language for the portal as a whole.
- It is not decided whether the gate at `/` offers *Step through* beside the key, or only `/guest` does.
- The guest room on Cloudflare is checked locally with stand-in models (`pnpm cloudflare:check`); its first real opening found a defect the check had not looked for (#24), so what the check does not open is not known to work.
- Measured once, not enough: `guest-docs/` with a real model (eight questions, three languages, one run); not measured: Spanish by eye, in the page and on a phone; Nova's one-step habit with a real model (it is only a line of her prompt); contrast of the faint text by skin; notifications and the icon's count on a real Android and iOS phone (the notice has been seen only in a browser with stand-ins); the snails in Bank and Radar by eye, on a phone, and the change from one snail to the next; the new labels and the spacing by eye in the three looks, and the foot of the side in a narrow window.

- A turn past the trail stays in the object's storage until the next check (about the least of ten minutes, `trailMinutes` and `forgetAfterMinutes`, and later by the delay of the alarm itself, which Cloudflare says can be a minute during maintenance or failover: a guide, not a ceiling): guests are not given it, but the team's readers of the guest room are, in that window.

## How this stays true

Whoever merges something that changes what the portal does or what comes next updates this file in the same sitting, or the next session does before anything else, and moves the stamp. This file keeps three kinds of things only: where we are, what is next, what is open. History goes to the changelog, procedures to their own pages.
