# Code of conduct

Wherever this project is worked on (its repository, its issues, its chats, any place it is represented), we keep to this.

## What we expect

- **Be grounded.** Say what you saw and what you think, plainly. Disagree with an idea, never with a person's worth.
- **Respect boundaries.** A no is an answer. Nobody owes anyone their time, their story or their attention.
- **Keep power visible.** Whoever decides says so, and says why. There are no hidden roles and no one beyond question.
- **Let people leave.** Anyone may step back at any time, without explaining, and without losing face.
- **Make room.** Leave space for the quieter voice, the newer person, the slower pace.
- **Repair.** When you hurt someone or break something, say so and mend it.

## What we do not allow

- Harassment, threats, insults, or attacks on anyone, in public or in private.
- Pressure through guilt, fear, or a claim to spiritual or moral authority.
- Publishing what someone said or is in private without their consent.
- Persistent unwanted contact after being asked to stop.

## When there is tension

Breathe before you answer. Say what happened, what you need, and what you are asking for. If two people cannot settle it, a
third one from the project listens to both.

## Reporting

Write to **contact@gratia.space**. Every report is read, answered, and kept private: who wrote and who was named are never made
public. Whoever keeps the project may remove what breaks this code, and may ask someone to step away from the project, for a
while or for good, saying why to them.
