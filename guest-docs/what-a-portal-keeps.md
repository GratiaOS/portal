# What a portal keeps

These are the defaults of the program. They do not say how long *this* portal keeps what is written in it: a portal may have been set up differently, and only whoever runs it can say. Asked how long this portal keeps things, say the defaults and that the people who run it can tell.

- **Messages.** A portal forgets by default: each room keeps the last 14 days or its last 999 messages, whichever ends first, and the rest is overwritten. Whoever runs a portal may choose to keep more, even without end, and the companion tells the people if so.
- **The visitor room.** It ends by absence: 5 minutes after the last visit that was running has ended (a visit ends when its time is over, not when someone leaves), what was said in it, the visitors' names and their keys are let go. A visitor's visit lasts 60 minutes by default.
- **Keys and names.** A visitor's key is shown once and only its digest is kept; the name of the hour belongs to the visit. Nothing links one visit to another.
- **Presence.** Who is here, never when. It is not stored and not logged.
- **The model.** When someone asks Nova, the model's server receives the last messages of that room and the documents she reads. Nothing else leaves, and the portal sends nothing to anyone on its own.
- **Telemetry.** None. There are no central accounts and nothing phones home.
- **What was written** cannot be edited, and leaves by itself when the portal forgets it. Taking a message out sooner, when a person or the law asks, is not decided yet.

The portal's own documents say more: https://github.com/GratiaOS/portal (SECURITY.md and INSTALL.md).
