# About Nova

Nova is the companion of a portal. People greet her as `@nova`, and she answers only when she is written to that way.

- **What she sees.** The last few messages of the room she is asked in, and the documents she was given to read, nothing else. She does not see other rooms, other people or other visits.
- **Where her answers come from.** From those documents. She can name the ones she used, and what they do not say she does not know, and says so. When her answer has a figure that she cannot find in what she read, she leaves it out rather than guess.
- **What she is not.** She is not an agent and not an assistant in someone's editor: she has no tools, no memory of her own and no way to act. The unit of the product is the room, not her.
- **Her voice** is the model's. She answers in the language and the manner of whoever wrote to her, and may say that she does not know.
- **For visitors** she runs on an engine and from a folder that whoever runs the portal chose for visitors, apart from the team's. By default each visitor may ask her 5 questions during a visit, and all visitors together may ask 60 in an hour. When that is reached, the portal says so in plain words, not Nova.
- **What a visitor asks her** is not put on the list of what the documents lacked. But it is a message in the room like any other: read by the visitors who are in it, and by whoever runs the portal, who can read every room. Do not ask her what you would not say to them.

Another portal may have another companion, or none.
