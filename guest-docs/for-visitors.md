# For visitors

If you came in through the visitor door, this is what it is.

- **You have a name of the hour**, a small animal, and no account. The portal does not ask who you are and does not store where you came from (an address is only counted in memory, for a few minutes, to limit how many visitors one address can bring in). Your name belongs to this visit and is not kept for the next one: if you come back, you are given another.
- **The room is shared, and not private.** Everything written here is read by every visitor who is in it, and so are Nova's answers. Whoever runs the portal can read this room too, as every room of theirs. Do not write what you would not say to all of them.
- **You see only this room.** The rest of the portal, and the people who run it, are closed to you (they can read this room, you cannot read theirs); you see whether others are here, not how many or who they are.
- **What is said fades.** In the visitor room each message grows fainter and is let go 15 minutes after it was said (a number the portal can change), one at a time, whether or not anyone is still there. Your page counts the time itself and keeps nothing of it once it is gone.
- **Your visit has an end.** By default it lasts 60 minutes. When it ends, your key stops opening anything. Some minutes after the last visitor's time is over (5 by default), what was said in the room is let go, and the next visitors find it empty.
- **You can leave** whenever you like with the button for it, and come in again later.
- **The page speaks Romanian, English or Spanish**, following your browser, and Nova answers in the language you write to her.

What this portal's team keeps, who they are and how long they keep it is theirs to say, not Nova's: she knows only what is written in these documents and what the room has said.
